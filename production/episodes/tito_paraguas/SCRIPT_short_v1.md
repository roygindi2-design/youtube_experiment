# SCRIPT — tito_paraguas: ¡Tito comparte su paraguas! ☔🐰 | #shorts

**Pattern:** `rescue_mission` · **Opening:** `first_raindrop_on_trunk` · **World:** rainy park: grey-blue rain, shiny puddles, a curving path with colorful flower beds, a green bench + the same park when the sun comes out with a big rainbow

**Experiment:** first sharing/kindness topic (RoboSquad 'Compartir es cuidar' 24.4M ×478) + first weather change inside a video (rain → sun + rainbow)

**Twist (visible):** the umbrella is too small for two, so Tito spreads his BIG EARS over the bunny like a second umbrella — an act of sharing only an elephant can do

**Moral:** ¡Compartir es más bonito!

## Story beats

1. A big raindrop lands on the tip of Tito's trunk, plic! It starts to rain (second 0)
2. Tito opens his bright yellow umbrella and walks proudly through the rainy park
3. Under a small leaf, a tiny grey bunny is soaking wet and shivering, ay, ay (someone needs help)
4. Tito holds his umbrella over the bunny: come here! (he shares)
5. The umbrella is small, so Tito spreads his BIG EARS over the bunny like a second umbrella (twist: only an elephant)
6. The sun comes out and a big rainbow appears over the park (reward)
7. Tito and the bunny splash together in a shiny puddle, splash! (fun)
8. Top-down: both under the yellow umbrella, the bunny snuggled at Tito's foot (moral + loop: a new drop falls)

## Spanish check (Mexico, ages 1–4)

- Every line 3–6 words, present tense, describes the picture; sound words repeat (¡splash!, ¡ñam!, ¡ay!, ¡plic!).
- Diminutives for warmth (ojitos, patita, colita, arbolitos, conejito); no Spain-only words.

## Changes 21.10 04:20 (producer feedback _general.md)

## Update 21.10 14:20 — liquid & life rules (producer _general.md)
- No frozen splash in a keyframe: frames show the moment BEFORE; the splash/flow happens in `motion`. Sleeping/static shots get life (breathing blanket, twinkling stars).

## Update 21.10 22:10 — face rules (researcher QC review)
- Close-up/medium motions end with: "Both eyes stay open and calm with the same gentle expression the whole time." (one-frame blinks/winks looked like popping eyes in ep2).
