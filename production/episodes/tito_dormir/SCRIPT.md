# SCRIPT — tito_dormir: ¡Tito tiene miedo de la sombra! 🌙🦒 | #shorts

**Pattern:** `trick_why` · **Opening:** `shadow_on_wall_question` · **World:** moonlit night bedroom: navy-blue walls with painted golden stars, round window with a big friendly moon, small wooden bed with a starry blanket, warm night-light + the view through the round window over sleepy hills

**Experiment:** first bedtime video + first 'trick_why' (visual proof by a mentor, More Good Stories ×759) — the shadow-puppet demonstration is the punchline

**Twist (visible):** Mamá lifts the plush giraffe in front of the lamp and the 'monster' shadow on the wall moves exactly with it — the viewer SEES that the scary shape was only the giraffe

**Moral:** ¡No es un monstruo, es tu jirafa! ¡A dormir, Tito!

## Story beats

1. Night. A big wobbly shadow with a long neck and two little horns appears on the bedroom wall; Tito peeks from under his blanket (problem in second 0)
2. Extreme close-up: Tito's eyes over the starry blanket, he pulls it up to his nose and calls ¡Mamá! (fear)
3. Mamá Elefanta comes in holding a small warm lamp (mentor arrives)
4. Mamá lifts the soft yellow plush giraffe in front of the lamp: the shadow on the wall moves with it, it is just the giraffe! (the trick that shows why)
5. Mamá makes a bunny shadow with her trunk and ears on the wall; Tito laughs (fear turns into play)
6. Tito lifts his trunk in the light and his own shadow is a big elephant going ¡tuuu! (he takes control)
7. Top-down: Tito cuddles the plush giraffe, Mamá tucks the starry blanket and kisses his forehead (reward)
8. Through the round window: the big moon shines, Tito sleeps smiling; the giraffe shadow on the wall now looks friendly (moral + loop)

## Characters

Tito (lock) + Mamá Elefanta (mama_lock, reuse_mama). One-off: soft yellow plush giraffe toy (no face), shadows.

- **cuarto:** A cozy child's bedroom at night, vertical 9:16 composition, camera at child eye level: navy-blue walls with small painted golden stars, a small wooden bed with a soft blanket covered in yellow stars and a round pillow, a little wooden shelf with a soft yellow plush giraffe toy, a round window showing a big friendly glowing moon, a small warm night-light glowing amber on the shelf, soft blue moonlight and warm amber light. Nobody is in the bedroom.
- **ventana:** A round wooden window seen from outside at night, vertical 9:16 composition, camera at child eye level: a big friendly glowing full moon above sleepy rounded blue hills, twinkling stars in a deep navy sky, a few glowing fireflies near the window, warm amber light inside the round window. Nobody is at the window.
