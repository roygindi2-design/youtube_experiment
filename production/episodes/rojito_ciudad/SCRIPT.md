# SCRIPT — rojito_ciudad: ¿Cómo hacen los carros de la ciudad? 🚌🚒 | #shorts

**Pattern:** `guess_interactive` · **Opening:** `sound_first_bus_honk` · **World:** toy city morning: a bus-stop street with a zebra crossing and a traffic light + a sandy construction pit beside a little railroad crossing

**Experiment:** NuNu 'X hace Y' sound song (NuNu ×95, Singaroo ×665): every lyric line is a DIFFERENT one-off vehicle friend doing exactly its sound on the beat; first multi-character sound song on the channel

**Twist (visible):** after five friends each make their sound, a new 'nii-noo!' is heard and nobody knows who it is — Rojito's own siren lights up FLASHING BLUE on his roof: the mystery sound was him

**Moral:** ¡Cada amigo tiene su sonido, y Rojito hace ¡nii-noo!

## Story beats

1. A big yellow bus honks PI-PI right next to Rojito at the bus stop: who made that sound? (question in second 0)
2. Close on the bus: its horn and big smiling mouth go pi, pi, pi (answer 1)
3. A green garbage truck lifts a bin and gobbles the trash: ñam, ñam, ñam (answer 2)
4. An orange excavator scoops sand in the pit: ras, ras, ras (answer 3)
5. A little blue train puffs past the crossing: chu, chu, chu (answer 4)
6. A white ambulance rolls by with its light flashing: ui, ui, ui (answer 5)
7. A new sound, nii-noo! Everyone looks around... it is Rojito's own siren lighting up (twist)
8. Rojito drives down the street spraying a water arc with a little rainbow, the bus honks again (reward + loop)

## Characters

Rojito (hero lock) + one-off friend vehicles, one per shot: yellow bus, green garbage truck, orange excavator, blue train, white ambulance (orange stripe, no red cross).

## Worlds

- **calle:** A cheerful toy city street on a sunny morning, outdoors, vertical 9:16 composition, camera low at toy level: a smooth grey play road with a white zebra crossing, a small bus stop with a blue roof and a bench, a friendly traffic light showing green, colorful rounded toy houses and shops in pastel orange, mint and yellow, little round trees, a wide open blue sky with white clouds. No vehicles on the road.
- **obra:** A sunny toy construction site outdoors, vertical 9:16 composition, camera low at toy level: a big pit of soft golden sand with orange and white traffic cones, a little red-and-white railroad crossing with a wooden barrier and a curved toy train track behind the pit, green rounded hills and a wide open blue sky with white clouds. No vehicles in the pit.
