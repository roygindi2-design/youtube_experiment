# SCRIPT — tito_ep3_dormir: ¿Qué es eso? ¡Tito no puede dormir! 🌙 + Más Canciones Infantiles

**Format:** long episode, 16:9 — song-story ~2:30 (new), then '+ más canciones' (compilation of earlier Tito songs) — total 8–12 min. Later 3 standalone Shorts are cut from it (9:16 crop from the center).
**Pattern:** `overcome_fear` · **Opening:** `bedtime_lights_off_click` · **BPM:** 108 · **song_seconds:** 150
**World:** starry night bedroom in 16:9: navy-blue walls with painted golden stars, a round window with a big friendly moon and a leafy branch, a small wooden bed, warm bedside lamp + the moonlit window seen from outside

**Moral:** ¡Con la luz, todo es amigo! Mira bien, respira despacito… y a dormir.

**Twist (visible):** every scary thing is something friendly once you LOOK with the light (shadow = plush giraffe, tapping = owl on a branch, two glowing eyes = headlights of his toy fire truck) — and in the third verse Tito turns on the light HIMSELF

## Why this episode (evidence — see research/14-longform-format.md)
- Fear of the dark / shadows won in 4 independent channels: TimoBoy '¡No Puedo Dormir!' long 15 min ×388 (~100K views/day), Pinkfong 'Monstruos de Sombras' 142K/day, Gracioso Bebé Shorts ×1,750 / ×1,496.
- Long-form 20–40 min = best median views/day (38K); '+ más' titles ×1.9 (longform scans 21.10).
- Structure = Monito y Canciones (3D, 79.5M in 3 months): one new habit song + compilation.

## Message (educational) — 3 tools a toddler can use
1. **Look with the light** — scary shapes are friendly things (shadow = toy, tapping = owl, eyes = headlights).
2. **Breathe slowly, 'uno, dos'** — calming (chorus line 3, shot K3).
3. **Be brave by yourself** — verses 1–2 Mamá turns on the light; verse 3 Tito does it himself.

## Sync rules for this song
- Every line = one shot = one action ON the beat; the sound word lands on the visual peak (¡clic! = button pressed, ¡toc, toc, toc! = 3 taps, ¡Uh-uh! = beak opens, ¡Nii-noo! = headlights glint).
- '¿Qué es eso? ¿Qué es eso?' is the SAME shot (N04) in verses 1–2 → ritual; in verse 3 it changes to N12 (Tito looks himself = growth).
- Chorus = the SAME 4 shots (K1–K4) all 3 times: trunk up ¡Tuuu! → hug → breathe → sleep. Children learn and copy the gesture.
- One cut per line (≈4.4 s at 108 bpm), no faster (Lillard & Peterson 2011; Google 'pleasant pace').
- Cold open (editor's option): 2 s of N03 (the huge shadow) + '¿Qué es eso?' BEFORE the intro, then the song from the top.

## Spanish check (Mexico, ages 1–4)
- **prender la luz** (Mexican; not 'encender'), **linterna**, **camioncito**, **lucecitas**, **chiquitito** — warm Mexican diminutives.
- **búho** + owl sound **¡Uh-uh!**; **¡A la cama!**; '¿Qué es eso?' repeated = easy for the child to shout.
- Lines 4–7 words; the chorus is identical 3 times (helps ACE-Step keep it, and children learn it).

## IP / safety check
- Plush giraffe is **mint-green with white spots** (a yellow giraffe with brown spots = the famous 'Sophie la girafe' toy look).
- Owl is a small fluffy BROWN owl with orange eyes (not a white snowy owl like Hedwig).
- The toy fire truck is a plain red toy (our own Rojito world), no face, no brand.
- Sweet, not scary: shadows are soft purple, no eyes in the shadow, warm light in every chorus.
