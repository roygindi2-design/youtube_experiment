# SCRIPT — tito_basura: ¡Tito, la basura no va ahí! 🦆🗑️ | #shorts

**Pattern:** `lesson_consequence` · **Opening:** `cold_open_toss_midair` · **World:** sunny duck-pond park: green lawn, wooden bench, weeping willow, lily pads, a family of yellow ducklings + a park path with a smiling green trash bin

**Experiment:** first littering/environment lesson (Milo Kids ×3,604, NursyTots ×1,382, VocaVoca 21.1M) with a visible consequence for a friend (sad duckling) and a talking trash bin as the fun fix

**Twist (visible):** Tito fixes his own mistake with his TRUNK: he reaches into the pond and lifts the dripping box out, and the trash bin 'eats' it with a ñam — the duckling's water turns sparkling

**Moral:** ¡La basura, a su lugar!

## Story beats

1. In the park Tito finishes his juice box and tosses it over his shoulder; it flies toward the pond (the mistake, second 0)
2. Splash! The juice box lands in the pond next to a fluffy yellow duckling, who jumps in surprise (consequence)
3. The duckling looks sadly at the floating box in its pond (feeling)
4. Mamá Elefanta points at the pond: Tito, where does trash go? Tito's trunk droops (mentor)
5. Tito reaches out with his long trunk and lifts the dripping juice box out of the water (twist: his trunk is the tool)
6. A smiling green trash bin opens its lid like a big mouth and gobbles the box: ñam, ñam, ñam! (fix)
7. The duckling family swims in sparkling clean water and quacks thank you (reward)
8. Tito and Mamá do a trunk high-five next to the bin (moral + loop)

## Characters

Tito (lock) + Mamá Elefanta (mama_lock, reuse_mama). One-off: yellow ducklings + mother duck, smiling green trash bin (face intended), plain white juice box (no face).
