**Pattern:** `rescue_mission` · **Opening:** `kitten_cry_high_branch` · **World:** sunny farm orchard: a very tall apple tree with red apples, a red barn and round golden hay bales + a dirt farm road between golden wheat fields

## Story beats

1. A tiny grey kitten clings to a branch high up in a tall apple tree and meows for help (emergency in second 0)
2. Rojito's siren flashes and he races down the farm road: nii-noo! (the rush)
3. He stops under the tree and looks up: the kitten is very, very high (the problem in scale)
4. His white ladder slides up and up until it touches the branch (the try)
5. At the top the kitten hugs the branch and shakes its head: it is too scared to climb down (obstacle)
6. The kitten slides down the slanted ladder like a slide, wiii! (twist: the ladder becomes a resbaladilla)
7. It lands in the soft hay next to its mother cat; both rub their heads on Rojito's bumper (thank you)
8. Top-down: the kitten runs straight back to the tree and starts climbing again, Rojito's eyes go wide (loop gag)

## Spanish check (Mexico, ages 1–4)

- **resbaladilla** = playground slide in Mexico (not 'tobogán', which is Spain).
- **No se quiere bajar** — natural spoken Mexican form; **¡Rojito ya va!** = 'Rojito is on his way'.
- **¡Sube y sube la escalera!** — describes the ladder going up and up (not an order to the child).
- Every line 4–6 words, with a sound word (¡miau!, ¡nii-noo!, ¡wiii!); one moral line, kept also in the picture (R7).
