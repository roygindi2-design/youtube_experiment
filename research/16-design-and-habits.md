# 16 — Richer scripts: design bible + habit beat (Roy 22.10)

Roy: scripts must say much more about the look and must contain small good-habit moments. A good-habit hit like handwashing should come back in other episodes in an elegant, small way. The Halloween set could be far richer. Rule: everything goes INTO the script, so the producer's prompts carry it.

## 1. Design bible (required section in every SCRIPT.md, before the timeline)
- **Environment**: per location, 8–12 concrete set-dressing items (furniture, props, decorations, textures), so prompts are never generic. Name colors.
- **Palette & light**: 3–5 main colors, plus light per story phase (e.g. lamp off = moonlit blue/lilac; lamp on = warm amber).
- **Costumes**: what every character wears in THIS episode (outfit fits the moment: pajamas at bedtime, raincoat in rain, costume at Halloween) + one accessory.
- **Supporting characters**: a short design sheet for each (species, size vs Tito, color, 1 signature detail, facial style = same cute 3D style, closed-mouth smile).
- **Background life**: 2–3 small living details per location (mobile turning, fireflies, leaves falling, curtain moving, steam from a cup), so no shot is ever frozen.
- **Hero props**: every object the story uses, with color/shape fixed (it must look the same in every shot).

## 2. Habit beat (one per episode, small, shown not preached)
One shot or one prop action that models a good habit. It is NOT the episode's topic, and it comes out of the story naturally.
| habit | evidence | elegant ways to show it |
|---|---|---|
| Handwashing | our ¡ALTO, Tito! ¿Y tus manos? idea; handwashing songs = evergreen top performers (research/11) | wash hands before eating candy / after mud / after the park |
| Brushing teeth | Doctora Sophia "Dulces de Halloween vs Comida Saludable \| Cepíllate los Dientes" 9.4M (Oct 2026) | toothbrush back in the cup before bed; brush after candy |
| Saying gracias / por favor | El Tren Ñam-Ñam manners 517K views/hour (22.10 momentum) | ¡Gracias! at each door; por favor when asking |
| Sharing | RoboSquad sharing ×478 (24.4M) | give one to a friend |
| Tidying up | "Cada juguete a su casita" (our own) | toy back in the box before leaving |
| Drinking water | mealtime winners (VocaVoca 659K/h) | a sip from a little cup after playing |

Rotation: don't repeat the same habit two episodes in a row, except handwashing (the strongest), which may come back every ~3 episodes.
