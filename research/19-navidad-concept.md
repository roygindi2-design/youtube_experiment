**Pattern:** guess what's inside (surprise) + a short, repeated chorus. Our Halloween door formula works in the same way → reuse the structure (3 verses, the same "¿Qué será?" question shot, chorus ×3).

## Concept: "¿Qué hay en la caja? 🎁 ¡Navidad con Tito!"
- **Setup:** Christmas Eve at home (Mexico: Nochebuena), a tree with lights, three wrapped gifts.
- **Verse 1:** a box that goes "¡tilín, tilín!" when shaken → ¿Qué será? → a little BELL (cascabel). Tito rings it.
- **Verse 2:** a soft, squishy box → ¿Qué será? → a warm red SCARF (bufanda). Tito puts it on.
- **Verse 3:** a big box with holes that moves… → ¿Qué será? → a star-shaped PIÑATA (Mexican posada tradition). Tito hits it with Mamá, candies rain down, and he GIVES them to his friends (the kitten, bat and mouse from Halloween = series continuity).
- **Chorus ×3 (signature dance):** "¡Tilín, tilín, Navidad! / ¡Abrazos y regalos! / ¡Dar es lo más bonito! / ¡Feliz, feliz Navidad!" — moves: bell shake (both hands), hug-self sway, offer a gift with both hands, jump for joy.
- **Moral (punchline):** "¡Dar es lo más bonito!" Giving > getting; Tito's own gift to Mamá at the end = a drawing he made (handmade).
- **Habit beat:** saying "¡gracias!" for each gift (a bow, the same gesture as the Halloween chorus).
- **Mexico check:** Nochebuena, posada piñata with 7 points, ponche (in the background), "cascabel", "bufanda"; no religious scenes (keeps it universal); Santa not needed.
- **Design ideas:** warm living room, tree with colored lights, papel picado navideño, poinsettias (nochebuena flowers), a window with soft snow (pretend, as on a Christmas card).
