## What they share (the common denominator)
1. **HOOK = the character is already IN the moment at frame 0** — never an establishing shot. Close-up of a face, or the action already happening (kiss, run, smash, kick, crying baby). Often a sound in the first 0.3 s: a baby voice ("Mama", "Papa"), a crunch, a "moo", a giggle.
2. **CONTENT = one tiny relatable family moment** (affection, mischief, a refusal, a small conflict between two characters) → a twist → a payoff (a laugh or a melt). Story in 3 beats: want → obstacle/twist → resolution. No lesson preached; when there's a moral (sharing), it's shown.
3. **TWO CHARACTERS** in almost every winner (kid + parent, kid + sibling, kid + animal). The relationship IS the content.
4. **EMOTION over information**: "cute/melt" or "relatable chaos". Parents share it ("send this to the dad who…" — @chikooaibaby).
5. **LOOP**: most are built to loop (ending flows into the start) → re-watches → watch time > 100%.
6. **LENGTH**: 7–20 s for the single-moment ones; longer only for compilations.
7. **TEXT**: parent-voice caption on screen ("Cuando…", "Mi hijo…", "POV…") on the live-action and some animated ones; many 3D ones use NO text, just a voice line.
8. **AUDIO**: starts instantly; a short voice line (baby word) + soft music, or SFX synced to the action. Songs only in the "dance/sound" formats.
9. **PRODUCTION**: Pixar-like 3D or AI 3D, warm light, close framing (faces big), expressive faces (the acting is the joke), 1 location, few cuts; static camera is fine. Not long — one gag.
10. **A recurring character** (Bobo the elephant, Nila & Diya, Bailey & Bean, Chikoo) → series habit, "full cartoon" follow-ups.

## Formats ranked for us (Tito = a baby elephant, Mamá Elefanta, friends)
1. **Tito & Mamá micro-moment** (7–12 s, loop): e.g., Tito says "¡Mamá!" and hugs her trunk / Tito runs from the bath towel / Tito hides the broccoli in his trunk / Tito wakes Mamá. ← closest to the 30–67M winners; cheapest (1–2 shots).
2. **Tito mischief + payoff** (12–18 s): Tito smashes a watermelon / splashes a puddle on Mamá / the last candy → he shares (Bobo ×33 + "last candy" ×43).
3. **Pop-in sounds/guessing** (10–15 s, loop): doors / animals pop in one by one with their sound ("¿Quién es?" — testing now).
4. Song/dance loops — weakest for parents; keep for YouTube.
Rules: frame 0 = face close-up already reacting + a sound; two characters; one gag; loop; ≤15 s; parent-voice text optional (A/B).
