# 06b — Web research: toddler Shorts hooks (2026-10-19, research agent)

Note: the agent could not open google.com / support.google.com / techcrunch.com directly; [YT] claims come from search results quoting YouTube's pages. **(folk)** = creator lore without official or peer-reviewed support.

## 1. What grabs a 2–4-year-old in seconds 0–3
- **Sound beats picture.** Sound effects, peculiar/child/female voices, sudden audio change and foreground music capture preschoolers' attention most reliably, even when they aren't looking (Anderson; PMC2936722).
- **Linked to more attention:** women's and children's voices, puppets, peculiar voices, movement, cuts, sound effects, laughter.
- **Linked to less attention:** adult male voices, long zooms or pans, **eye contact** (character staring at the viewer), still shots (Alwitt, Anderson et al. 1980).
- **Comprehensibility drives attention.** Pair one salient sound with one clear, simple image (PMC3305831).
- **Familiar tunes hold attention better.** Evidence is from infants, 2022 (U Toronto).
- **On-screen "talking to the child" is weak by itself.** Responsive cues alone did not help toddlers learn (Troseth 2019).
- **Fast-paced editing harms short-term self-control in 4-year-olds** (Lillard & Peterson, Pediatrics 2011).

## 2. Length and loops
- About 15–35 s is the third-party sweet spot. No kids-specific study exists.
- Since 31 Mar 2025 every start or replay counts as a view [YT]. Judge Shorts by "Engaged views", "Stayed to watch" and average % viewed.
- Seamless loops as a ranking signal: (folk). Still, a loop pushes average % viewed above 100% at no cost.

## 3. New channels and the Shorts feed
- From YouTube, via Creator Insider (Aug 2023) [YT]:
  - Upload time doesn't matter for non-news Shorts.
  - Posting frequency is not a factor.
  - **Don't delete and re-post a Short; it may be treated as spam.**
- Seed audience, then expand if few viewers swipe away (vidIQ, third-party). Views often arrive days later.
- Made for Kids removes comments, notifications, personalized ads, miniplayer and save-to-playlist [YT].
  - No official statement that MFK reduces Shorts reach.
  - Shorts reportedly aren't shown in the YouTube Kids app (Qustodio).
- Oct 2026: YouTube said it would cut the reach of re-uploaded or aggregated clips. Original animation is fine.

## 4. Upload time
- YouTube: doesn't matter for Shorts [YT].
- Agent recommendation: one fixed slot, about 20:00 UTC (14:00 Mexico City). This removes time as a variable.

## 5. Titles, hashtags, first frame
- The feed shows the video, not the title. The title still matters for search, home, the channel page and topic understanding.
- Use a short Spanish title naming the topic, plus 2–4 hashtags.
- The title must match what the video actually teaches.
- The first frame acts as the thumbnail outside the feed.

## 6. Formats for a 20–30 s song
- **Good fits:** peekaboo; animal-sound riddles (sound first); colour change; counting 1–5; one routine shown as 3 steps.
- **Avoid:** surprise eggs and toy boxes (unboxing is a YouTube low-quality signal).
- **Folk-song idea:** "Un elefante se columpiaba", a traditional Latin American rhyme about an elephant. Confirm it is public domain and use an original arrangement.

## 7. Hard NO list: YouTube kids & family quality principles
Low quality includes:
- Commercial or product focus, unboxing.
- Bad behaviour.
- Deceptive educational framing: wrong colours or counts, or a title that doesn't match the video.
- Hard to follow, unclear audio.
- Sensational or misleading titles or thumbnails.
- Strange use of children's characters.
- Mass-produced, repetitive template output.

Consequences: less reach, removal from YouTube Kids, limited ads, YPP risk.

## Top rules applied to the sprint
1. Sound first: a distinctive sound at 0.0–0.3 s, and the melody by 1 s.
2. One clear bright image with movement. No still opening shot, no long zoom.
3. Female or child singing voice.
4. 20–30 s, last frame matching the first (loop).
5. Never delete or re-post. Colours and titles must be accurate. No toys or products.
6. Change only the hook between tests.
