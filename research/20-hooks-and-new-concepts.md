## Concept A — "¡Las Puertas Mágicas de Tito! 🚪❄️☀️🌧️" (rewrite of tito_frio_calor) — top pick
- **Hook (0 s):** in Tito's house, three colored doors appear in the wall with sparkles. "¿Qué hay detrás de la puerta?"
- **Verse 1:** the BLUE door → a snowy world: snow falls on Tito, "¡Brrr!", he shivers in his overalls. ¿Qué me pongo? → a hat, scarf and jacket fly to him (magic) → he slides down a snowy hill on a sled (ACTION).
- **Verse 2:** the YELLOW door → the beach, a hot sun, "¡Uf, qué calor!" while he's still in the jacket (the joke) → he takes it off, a sun hat, sunscreen, water → he surfs a little wave with a friendly dolphin (ACTION).
- **Verse 3:** the GREEN door → a rainy jungle, "¡plic, plic!" → a raincoat + boots → he jumps in puddles, and a little froggy friend is getting wet → Tito holds his umbrella over the frog (helping others, hidden value).
- **Chorus ×3 (signature dance):** "¡Abro la puerta, ¿qué será?! / ¡Frío, calor o lluvia, ya! / ¡Me visto bien y a jugar! / ¡Tito sabe qué usar!" — moves: knock-knock, peek, a spin to "change clothes", jump for joy.
- **Punchline:** the last (RED) door opens… onto his home, Mamá with open arms: "¡Y aquí, un abrazo!" — the best world is home.
- **Learning:** clothes for the weather (frío/calor/lluvia) + colors of the doors. **Hidden value:** shelter a friend from the rain.

## Concept B — "¡Rojito y las Ruedas Locas! 🚒⭕🔺⬛" (rewrite of rojito_arena)
- **Hook (0 s):** ¡Nii-noo! A kitten is stuck in a tree, Rojito races off… BUMP! A wheel pops off and rolls away.
- **Verse 1:** a SQUARE wheel arrives → "¡Pum, pum, pum!" Rojito bumps along, can't go → "¿Esa no es! ¿Cuál es?" (the kids shout).
- **Verse 2:** a TRIANGLE wheel → "¡Clonc, clonc!", he wobbles → no!
- **Verse 3:** a STAR wheel → "¡Brinca, brinca!" he bounces → no! → then the ROUND wheel → "¡Zuuum!" he races.
- **Chorus ×3:** "¡Rueda, rueda, redondita! / ¡Gira, gira, rapidita! / ¿Cuál es la que va? / ¡Redonda, redonda, ya!"
- **Punchline + ACTION:** he arrives just in time, his ladder goes up and up, the kitten jumps into a little basket on the ladder → hug. A final gag: the kitten wants a ride and honks the horn.
- **Learning:** shapes (circle, square, triangle, star). **Hidden value:** helping others (a rescue), patience (try again).
- Evidence: Car Wheel Español ×4 videos 187–543K/day with exactly this puzzle; rescue trend.

## Concept C — "¡Súper Tito al Rescate! 🦸🐘☔" (rewrite of tito_paraguas)
- **Hook (0 s):** a thunder rumble, rain; a tiny duckling is crying under a leaf. Tito puts on a red cape (a towel tied by Mamá) → "¡Súper Tito!"
- **Verse 1:** a duckling getting wet → Super Tito spreads his BIG EARS like an umbrella over it (his superpower!).
- **Verse 2:** a little bunny's carrot is stuck in a puddle → Super Tito sucks up the water with his trunk and sprays it away (a water-hose superpower, ACTION).
- **Verse 3:** a baby bird fell out of its nest → Super Tito lifts it gently back with his trunk, the mother bird sings.
- **Chorus ×3:** "¡Súper Tito, ya llegó! / ¡Con orejas y trompón! / ¡Ayudar es lo mejor! / ¡Súper, súper corazón!" — moves: hands on hips (hero pose), a cape twirl, ear flap, heart hands.
- **Punchline:** the rain stops, a rainbow appears, all the friends make a heart around Tito… and Mamá calls "¡Súper Tito, a cenar!" — he runs home, the cape flying.
- **Learning/value:** helping others, every body part is special (ears, trunk). **Hidden:** caring for nature (the bird nest).
- Evidence: superhero-rescue (Monito, Bebefinn, BabyBus 100–390K/day); sharing (Bang Bang Monkey 1.78M/day).
