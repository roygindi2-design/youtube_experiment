# 18 — TikTok lessons from the first video (pond/ducks Short, 22.10)
Data: research/tiktok/analytics_2026-10-22.json — 548 views in ~19 h, 5 likes, 2 saves, 3 followers. **Avg watch 3.8 s of 32 s; 0.88% watched to the end.**

## What it means
1. **Distribution works, retention doesn't.** TikTok gave a new account 548 views with no followers. But people swipe away after ~4 s, so TikTok stops pushing. The first 2 seconds decide everything.
2. **The viewers are parents, not kids**: 25–34 = 49%, 18–44 = 85%. 84% are in Nicaragua/Honduras/El Salvador. The hook must speak to a PARENT scrolling on their own: a relatable kid problem, a cute surprise, or a "watch this with your kid" game.
3. **Too long for TikTok.** 32 s with a slow start = 0.88% completion. TikTok cuts must be 12–20 s and start mid-action.

## Rules for TikTok cuts (separate from YouTube Shorts)
- 12–20 s. Frame 0 = the most surprising/cute moving moment (knock + "?" / reveal / splash), never a calm establishing shot.
- **On-screen text hook allowed on TikTok** (it isn't YouTube MFK): one short line in the first 0–2 s, for parents: "¿Quién está detrás de la puerta? 👀", "Cuando tu peque no quiere bañarse 😅", "Juega a adivinar con tu peque 👶".
- End on a loop point (the last frame flows into the first) → replays count and raise completion.
- Caption = question + 3–5 hashtags in Spanish (#cancionesinfantiles #mamás #crianza #halloween…). The YouTube link goes in the bio.
- Measure each TikTok: avg watch ≥ 6 s and completion ≥ 15% = good; otherwise the next one changes the hook.
