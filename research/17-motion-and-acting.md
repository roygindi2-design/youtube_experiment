Research: by ~18 months ~80% of infants add imitated gestures to their dancing (Developmental Psychology 2023); imitation of musical gestures in sync with the adult/character is how toddlers join in (Frontiers in Psychology 2020).

**Takeaway:** what wins is not complex animation but ONE clear, copyable move per line, done ON the beat, repeated identically (the chorus), plus readable faces (happy squint, surprise "o", proud, no-no). Big body moves for joy, small precise ones for meaning.

## The Tito move library (use these names + exact wording in prompts)
Write each shot's motion as: **START pose → ACTION (on beat …) → END pose; secondary motion; what stays still.** One main action per 5 s clip, plus at most one secondary.
| name | exact description for the prompt |
|---|---|
| TUUU | starts standing, trunk down; raises his trunk straight up in one smooth move on the beat, holds it up for one beat while his ears lift, then lowers it back down below his mouth; feet stay planted |
| HAPPY BOUNCE | bends both knees and bounces softly up and down on every beat, arms loose, ears flopping a little with each bounce; stays on the same spot |
| SWAY | sways his whole body slowly left, then right, in time with the music, head tilting with the sway; feet stay planted |
| CLAP-CLAP | brings both hands together in front of his tummy twice on the beat, smiling with closed mouth |
| EAR FLAP | flaps both big ears forward and back twice, like waving them, head still |
| FREEZE | moving happily, then stops completely still on the beat in a fun pose (one arm up), holds for one beat, then relaxes |
| JUMP FOR JOY | crouches a little, jumps up with both arms raised, lands softly on both feet, ears bounce |
| SPIN | turns around once in place, slowly, arms out, and ends facing the camera |
| NO-NO | shakes his head slowly left-right twice, ears swinging, closed-mouth frown (✗ moment) |
| THUMBS-UP | lifts one hand with a thumbs up next to his cheek, proud closed smile (✓ moment) |
| PEEKABOO | covers his eyes with both hands, then opens them wide to the camera with a surprised smile |
| TIPTOE | walks slowly on tiptoes across the frame, knees high, hands up near his chest, sneaky |
| HUG-SELF | wraps both arms around himself, sways gently, eyes softly closed, happy |
| REAL WALK | walks forward with small steps while the camera tracks backwards and the houses pass by (or he crosses the frame left→right) |

## Face library (no teeth, ever)
| name | exact description |
|---|---|
| HAPPY SQUINT | eyes close into soft upside-down U shapes, cheeks lift, closed-mouth smile |
| SURPRISE O | eyes wide, eyebrows up, mouth a small round closed "o" |
| CURIOUS | head tilts 15° to one side, one ear lifts, eyes look toward the thing |
| PROUD | chin up, chest out, closed smile, eyes bright |
| SHY | looks down, small smile, hands together, ears droop slightly |
| SAD-SMALL | ears droop, eyebrows tilt up in the middle, lower lip pushed out slightly, no tears |
| SLEEPY | heavy half-closed eyes, slow blink, closed-mouth smile (never a big open yawn) |

## Rules
- Chorus = one named dance, 4 moves (one per line), identical ×3 → the "signature dance" kids copy.
- Each verse has ≥1 big-body moment (bounce/jump/spin) and ≥1 precise meaning gesture (point, no-no, thumbs-up).
- Sound words land on the move peak (¡Tuuu! = trunk at the top).
- Never two main actions in one 5 s clip; never an open-mouth laugh/yawn.
