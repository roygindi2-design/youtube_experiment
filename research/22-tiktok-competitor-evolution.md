## D. What separates them
1. **A job for the parent.** SKZ = "my toddler learns animal sounds and imitates them" → saves (11.5K) + comments (kids' parents reply). YumYum = nothing to do → 0 comments.
2. **Predictable call → response rhythm.** Toddlers anticipate "who's next?"; babies/pre-verbal kids loop it. Length isn't a problem when the rhythm is the content: 24–72 s won here, versus our 7–15 s gag rule for story clips (research/21). That's two different genres.
3. **Iteration + a ruthless double-down.** SKZ tried ~10 themes, saw one outlier, tested it 5–6 more times, then dropped everything else. Every later tweak stayed inside the winner (baby + adult → 1M; compilation length → 1.5M). YumYum posted 107 copies of one idea with zero variation.
4. **Search captions.** SKZ: "LEARN ANIMAL SOUNDS FOR KIDS | DOG SAYS WOOF…", keyword-dense and searchable. YumYum: hashtags only.
5. **Frame 0 = one cute animal, big, centered, clean background, sound in ≤1–2 s.** This matches research/18.
